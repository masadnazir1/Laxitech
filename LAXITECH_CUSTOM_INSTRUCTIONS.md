# LAXITECH / GALAXYDEV — CUSTOM INSTRUCTION & SYSTEM DESIGN GUIDE

> **Mandate:** High-craft, minimalist, conversion-focused enterprise cloud platform for **Laxitech Technologies Inc. & GalaxyDev PK** (`galaxydev.pk` / `cloud.galaxydev.pk`).
> **Core Principle:** "Less is more. Minimalism is better than maximalism." Do NOT complicate things for clients. Everything must be effortless, crystal clear, responsive, and aligned with our existing Cynex design system and 21st.dev component patterns.

---

## 1. Visual & Architectural Foundations
1. **Color Palette:**
   - Background: Deep Obsidian (`--vv-bg`, `#05070E` / `#0B0F19`)
   - Accent Primary: Electric Blue (`#0066FF`)
   - Accent Secondary: Cyber Violet (`#7C3AED` / `#8B5CF6`)
   - Foreground Text: Titanium White (`#FFFFFF`) & Crisp Muted (`#94A3B8`)
   - Specular Border: 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with traveling conic shine
2. **Typography System:**
   - Display Headings: Walsheim Sans (`font-display`, -0.04em tracking, tight leading 0.95–1.05)
   - Editorial Accents: Fraunces Serif (`font-serif`, italic highlights)
   - Body & Controls: Inter / Geist Sans (15–16px, readable line-height 1.6)
   - Data & Terminals: JetBrains Mono (numbers, latencies, pings, code)
3. **No-Slop & Zero-Confusion Rules:**
   - NO arbitrary fake agency items (e.g. "Movix Media", "SuperLuxuries", fashion boutiques). All products are authentic Laxitech / GalaxyDev cloud infrastructure.
   - NO cluttered cards or random decorative blobs. Every element serves client decision-making.
   - NO multi-paragraph walls of text. Clean headlines (<=2 lines), sharp value propositions, instant CTAs.
